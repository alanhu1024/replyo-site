Replyo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Replyo is an independent browser extension that shows the directly replied-to post beside supported replies in X notifications. This policy explains how Replyo handles information. Contact: alanhu1024@gmail.com.

What Replyo processes

After you review the data-use notice and enable Replyo, it reads information from supported X pages in your browser: post and reply text, author display names and usernames, post identifiers and links, and the relationship between a reply and its direct parent. It also reads the current X account handle from the navigation profile link to keep context caches separate when accounts change.

This includes website content and personal information such as names and usernames. Processing happens locally even though this information is not sent to the developer. Replyo does not read X direct messages, your password, or authentication cookies. It does not read Chrome's history database or track activity across other websites. It handles the specific X post links needed for its context feature.

Why it uses this information

Replyo uses this information only to identify a reply's direct parent, display its text and author, reuse recent context, and avoid mixing information between signed-in accounts. It does not use it for advertising, profiling, AI training, or unrelated purposes.

Loading missing context

Previously cached context can be displayed automatically. If context is missing, manual loading is the default. When you choose to load it, Replyo may open a temporary inactive X tab to read the relevant parent page. You can separately enable automatic loading in the popup. It loads one page at a time. It closes its own loading tab after completion, failure, or timeout if you have not taken it over and it is still on the requested post page or has been redirected to an X sign-in page. Tabs you take over or navigate elsewhere are kept open.

These page requests go directly from your browser to X over HTTPS using your existing X session. X may receive normal website request information, such as the requested page and network information, under its own practices. Normal browser history behavior may also apply to these visits. Replyo does not copy or send your login credentials to the developer.

Storage and retention

  • Preferences: The enabled and automatic-loading settings are stored in chrome.storage.local. They remain until you change them or uninstall Replyo; they are not synchronized through Chrome Sync by the extension.
  • Context cache: Recent post context is stored in chrome.storage.session and separated by the identified X account. Records stop being used after 15 minutes and are removed at the next cache operation. There is no background timer guaranteeing physical deletion at exactly 15 minutes. The cache is limited to 300 records and 4 MiB overall, and can be evicted earlier. It is cleared when the browser session ends.
  • Temporary loading state: While loading context, session storage also records the extension-created tab identifier, the requested parent-post identifier, and whether the tab has been taken over. These records support safe cleanup, are removed when loading ends, and do not form a list of your other tabs.
  • Session coordination: A numeric cache-revision counter synchronizes cache clearing and pausing across extension components. It contains no post content or account identifier and is cleared when the browser session ends.
  • Clear cache: You can delete the current context cache from the Replyo popup at any time. Content on an open X page remains governed by X and is not deleted by this action.

Replyo does not keep an archive of your conversations or a copy on a developer server.

Sharing and developer access

Replyo has no developer-operated backend, analytics service, advertising tracker, or AI service. The extension does not transmit cached posts, account handles, or preferences to the developer or sell them to anyone. Apart from the direct X requests needed to load context, Replyo does not transfer this information to another service.

If you voluntarily email support, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. The email provider processes that correspondence. It is used to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue, retained only as needed for that purpose and any applicable obligations, and deleted on request when it no longer needs to be kept. Please avoid sending passwords, authentication cookies, or private post content. Support does not require access to your X account.

Your controls

You can enable or disable Replyo, choose manual or automatic loading, clear its context cache, or uninstall it. Turning it off stops new content processing and clears the context cache. Uninstalling removes the extension's local settings and storage. For support correspondence or privacy questions, email alanhu1024@gmail.com.

Security and limitations

Replyo requests access only to https://x.com/* and uses packaged extension code. Its context cache is temporary browser-session storage; this policy does not claim that cached data is separately encrypted by Replyo. Other people or software with access to your browser or device may be able to access information displayed there. X controls the source content and its availability.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Replyo's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Information handled for context previews is used only for that disclosed feature. It is not sold, used for personalized advertising, or used to determine creditworthiness or lending eligibility.

This policy page and changes

The packaged policy page contains no scripts, external fonts, or tracking resources. If you view a hosted copy, your browser contacts that website's hosting provider; this is separate from the extension's operation.

The policy may be updated when Replyo changes. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes to data handling will be disclosed in the extension before the changed handling begins, with consent obtained where required.
